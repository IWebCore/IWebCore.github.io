    I am married and have a child who is nearly three years old.

    I am currently in the job-seeking phase, hoping to find a suitable, long-term C++ position in Hefei.

    I am proficient in C++ development and Qt development, having worked on or independently developed multiple software applications in this regard.

    • I am familiar with reading cppreference documentation and actively stay updated with the latest C++ standards and proposals, proactively learning various aspects of C++.
    • Regarding Qt, I have delved into its kernel features, studied Qt source code, and read QtDoc help documentation, making me quite knowledgeable about Qt content.
    • I have a good understanding of various C++ knowledge areas and can apply them effectively.
    • For projects, I am experienced with project management and configuration tools like qmake and cmake, capable of managing large-scale projects with these tools.
    • I can perform code optimization, project refactoring, and enhance software quality.
    • I am skilled in project architecture design and functional implementation.

    Reading¶

    I enjoy reading books. The image below shows the books I have read in a corner of my home; some are not displayed here. The scale of technical documents, e-books, and library books I have read is similar, though I haven't kept detailed statistics.

    Most of these books are technical, covering areas such as C++, image processing, project management, etc.

    Learning¶

    I am self-directed and enthusiastic about learning. For example, when I struggled to master CMake, I dedicated a period of time to reading three CMake books in Chinese. One was already published, and the other two were translated by Li Xiaowei (one of his translations was even shared privately by the author after receiving a warning). Additionally, I studied CMake's built-in help documentation and parts of CMakeLists.txt files. This doesn't mean I can write CMake code with my eyes closed, but it means I have a solid grasp of CMake and can use it without difficulty.

    Another example is when using Qt's meta-object system; I focused on studying the code because there was no relevant documentation available.
